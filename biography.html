Luca Pollastri is a software engineer with a passion for building robust, maintainable systems. He has contributed to open-source projects and brings both depth and practicality to every problem he approaches.

Biography

Luca grew up with a curiosity for how computers work, leading him to pursue a career at the intersection of software craftsmanship and systems thinking. Over the years he has worked across backend development, developer tooling, and automation.

He believes in writing code that is easy to reason about, prioritises clarity over cleverness, and values the long-term health of a codebase as much as short-term velocity.

When not in front of a terminal, Luca enjoys cycling through the Italian countryside, experimenting with espresso recipes, and reading about the history of computing.
