Meet Our Executive Leaders

Amadeus IT Group's Executive Committee brings together diverse expertise across technology, finance, travel, hospitality, and corporate affairs to drive innovation across more than 190 countries.

Luis Maroto Camino

President & Chief Executive Officer

CEO of Amadeus since January 2011, Luis has led the company through two decades of global expansion in travel technology. He previously served as Deputy CEO (2009–2010) and CFO (2003–2009), making him one of the most tenured leaders in the travel-tech industry.

CEO Strategy Since 2003

Carol Borg

SVP & Chief Financial Officer

Carol joined Amadeus as CFO in May 2025, bringing extensive financial leadership experience. She previously served as CFO at QinetiQ Group PLC and Lightsource bp, and brings a strong track record in international finance and capital markets.

CFO Finance Since 2025

Sylvain Roy

SVP & Chief Technology Officer

CTO since January 2023 and President of Amadeus France, Sylvain leads Amadeus' technology strategy and a global engineering community of over 10,000 people across 50+ sites. He began his Amadeus journey in 2003 as a C++ programmer and studied mathematics and computer science at the École Polytechnique, Paris.

CTO Engineering Cloud & AI

Ana Doval de las Heras

SVP, People, Culture, Communication & Brand

Ana joined Amadeus in 2022 as the company's Chief Human Resources Officer, overseeing people strategy, culture, internal communications, and brand. She champions Amadeus' commitment to diversity, inclusion, and employee development across its global workforce.

CHRO People & Culture Since 2022

Decius Valmorbida

SVP & President, Travel Unit

President of the Travel Unit since August 2020 and Executive Committee member since 2017, Decius leads the Airlines, Travel Channels, and Airports businesses. He steers Amadeus' platform-led approach to serve travel sellers and airlines around the world.

Travel Unit Airlines Since 2017

Francisco "Paco" Pérez-Lozao Rüter

SVP & President, Hospitality

Paco leads Amadeus' Hospitality business unit, overseeing approximately 3,000 employees and operations spanning 170 countries. Under his leadership, Amadeus has become a major provider of technology solutions for hotels, property management, and the broader hospitality ecosystem.

Hospitality Hotels 170 Countries

Jackson Pek

SVP & Chief Corporate and Legal Affairs Officer

SVP & General Counsel since 2022, Jackson first joined Amadeus in 2006 and served as General Counsel for Asia Pacific for 12 years. After a stint as Chief Legal Officer at AirAsia Group, he returned to Amadeus in 2020 and now oversees all legal, regulatory, and corporate affairs globally.

General Counsel Legal Corporate Affairs

Rongrong "Vvivi" Hu

SVP, Corporate Strategy

Rongrong joined the Executive Committee effective January 2026 as SVP of Corporate Strategy. She brings extensive international experience in strategy, planning, and innovation across multiple geographies, and reports directly to the President & CEO.

Strategy Innovation Since 2026
