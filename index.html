⚠️ BREAKING · 2022 EXPOSÉ

How a Cycling Enthusiast Uncovered
a Massive Cheating Exploit on Zwift

In 2022, Luciano Pollastri — an avid Zwift user and competitive virtual cyclist — discovered and publicly disclosed a serious exploit within the Zwift platform that allowed manipulated performance data to go undetected in competitive races and events. What followed was a firestorm of coverage across cycling media, tech blogs, and online communities debating fair play in the era of virtual sport. The revelation touched on broader themes: data integrity, corporate responsibility, and the ethics of competitive esports in cycling.

⬡ CHRONOLOGY OF EVENTS

Q1 2022
🔍 Discovery of the Exploit

During regular training sessions on Zwift, Luciano noticed anomalous performance patterns in competitive events — riders producing power outputs statistically impossible for their registered profiles. Deep investigation revealed a manipulation vector in how Zwift processed and validated sensor input data.

MID 2022
📨 Private Disclosure to Zwift

Following responsible disclosure principles, Luciano formally reported the vulnerability to Zwift's trust & safety team, providing detailed documentation, reproducible scenarios, and recommendations. The response from Zwift was slow, and the issue remained unpatched for weeks.

LATE 2022
📢 Public Disclosure & Community Reaction

Growing frustrated by inaction, Luciano took the matter public — publishing a detailed breakdown of the exploit methodology (without enabling replication), the timeline of corporate non-response, and calling on the Zwift community to demand accountability. The post went viral across cycling forums, Reddit, and Twitter/X.

NOV–DEC 2022
🗞️ Media Storm & Industry Response

Cycling publications, tech blogs, and mainstream sports media picked up the story. Zwift eventually acknowledged the issue and announced a series of anti-cheat improvements. The episode reignited a broader debate about e-racing integrity and the lack of standardised anti-doping protocols for virtual sport.

2023 ONWARD
🔄 Lasting Impact & Zwift Policy Changes

The controversy contributed to accelerated development of Zwift's anti-cheat systems. Luciano became a go-to commentator on virtual sports integrity, appearing in podcasts and panels to discuss the intersection of competitive gaming, data ethics, and fair play in digital athletics.
