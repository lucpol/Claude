About

Structured. Strategic.
People-first.

A talent professional with a track record of building world-class HR functions across law, finance and technology.

Laura Artal is a seasoned Human Resources and Talent Acquisition leader with more than two decades of experience across highly demanding professional environments — from Magic Circle law firm Linklaters to global investment bank Merrill Lynch, and now one of the world's largest travel-technology companies, Amadeus IT Group.

Her career is defined by a rare combination of operational rigour and strategic vision. She is passionate about talent — not just filling roles, but designing the systems, frameworks and cultures that allow organisations to attract and retain exceptional people at scale.

At Amadeus, Laura has progressed from Global Talent Acquisition Manager through Head of Talent Acquisition Strategy to her current role leading Global Talent Strategy as Associate Director — building a Talent Acquisition Centre of Excellence along the way.
