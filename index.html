Chairman & CEO

Patrick Pouyanné

Chairman and Chief Executive Officer

Born June 24, 1963 (French). Patrick Pouyanné has been Chairman and CEO of TotalEnergies since 2015, steering the company's strategic pivot from a pure-play oil major toward a diversified energy company with a growing focus on renewables, gas, and low-carbon solutions. He chairs the Strategy & CSR Committee and is a graduate of École Polytechnique and Corps des Mines.
